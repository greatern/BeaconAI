# 🚨 Beacon AI

> **The Waze for Community Intelligence**

Beacon AI is an AI-powered community intelligence platform that helps South African citizens stay informed about risks in their surrounding environment through real-time reporting, machine learning, predictive analytics, and geospatial intelligence.

Instead of waiting for problems to escalate, Beacon AI enables communities to identify, verify, predict, and visualize incidents before they become major disruptions.

---

## 🌍 Why Beacon AI?

South Africans face daily challenges such as:

- 🚧 Potholes
- ⚡ Power outages
- 💧 Water leaks
- 🌊 Flooding
- 🚦 Broken traffic lights
- 🗑 Illegal dumping
- 🚔 Crime hotspots
- 🌳 Fallen trees
- 🚑 Road accidents

Most reporting platforms are reactive, fragmented, or municipality-specific.

Beacon AI aims to become a citizen-first platform that combines community reporting with artificial intelligence to provide actionable insights and predictive community intelligence.
